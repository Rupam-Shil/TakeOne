# Code of Conduct

Take One follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces is expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and kind, especially to people who are new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Disagree respectfully and accept decisions gracefully, even when they don't go your way.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualised content, or publishing anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable or breaks these rules, report it privately to the maintainer, [@Rupam-Shil](https://github.com/Rupam-Shil), using the private reporting form on this repository: **Security → Report a vulnerability**. Start the title with "Conduct" so it's clear it isn't a security issue. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.

The maintainer will review every report and may respond with anything from a private conversation to a permanent ban, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
